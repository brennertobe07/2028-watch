News (7 days) is the number of distinct Google News stories in the last 7 days that name the candidate with 2028 or presidential-run terms. The feed returns about 100 stories per search, so treat it as a relative measure of buzz, not an exact count. Change compares against the count from 7 days earlier and fills in once a week of history exists.

Virginia (30 days) counts stories whose headline names both the candidate and a Virginia place or figure. This filter is strict on purpose: it is meant to flag visits, endorsements and other in-state activity, not every passing mention. Poll avg is the mean of the national primary polling aggregators listed on Wikipedia. The roster and status (Declared / Exploratory / Interested / Speculated) come from Wikipedia's 2028 primaries pages. A dash means no coverage or no polling.
